# 💰 KK's Koala Tycoon: Monetization Strategy & Feature Ideas

This document serves as the master catalog for revenue design, GamePasses, Developer Products, in-game currency sinks, and Robux monetization strategies for **KK's Koala Tycoon**.

---

## 🎯 Monetization Design Philosophy
1. **Fair & Rewarding Free-to-Play:** All core tycoon progression (exhibit building, koala rescue, growing, basic terminal analytics) is achievable with in-game Cash.
2. **Quality-of-Life & Time Savers:** Paid features provide convenience, automation, enhanced remote access, and aesthetic flair rather than punitive paywalls.
3. **Collectible Appeal:** High-rarity koalas and prestige outfits create natural desire for cosmetics and rescue boosts.

---

## 🖥️ 1. Zookeeper Terminal & Analytics Monetization

### A. Cash vs. Robux Access Tiers
* **In-Game Cash (Current Implementation):**
  * **Zookeeper Computer Unlock:** \$350 Cash (one-time).
  * **Exhibit Telemetry Module:** \$250 Cash per exhibit.
  * **Happiness Diagnostics Module:** \$200 Cash per exhibit.
* **Timed Diagnostic Leases (Future Cash/Robux hybrid):**
  * Players can buy a **15-minute Live Diagnostic Surge** for a lower Cash fee (or small Robux spend).
  * Perfect for players troubleshooting their park during expansion before committing to permanent upgrades.

### B. "Zoo Director's Tablet" (Premium GamePass)
* **Concept:** Instead of walking back to the Vet Clinic office to view the computer, players receive an interactive handheld **Director Tablet** tool in their inventory.
* **Features:**
  * **Open Terminal Anywhere:** Instant access from anywhere on the island.
  * **Permanent Global Telemetry:** Automatically unlocks Telemetry and Diagnostics for all current and future exhibits.
  * **Push Notifications:** Alert banners when an exhibit's food level falls below 20% or an animal enters a "Grumpy" mood.
  * **Remote Rename:** Rename exhibits and inspect animals on the go.

## 🐨 2. Rescue Forest & Gacha Monetization

### A. Golden & Lucky Milk Bottles (Developer Products / GamePass)
* **Lucky Milk Bottle (Dev Product):**
  * Rolls wild joeys with boosted rarity rates (e.g., Cute: 60%, Super Cute: 30%, Ultra Cute: 10% vs default 90/8/2%).
* **Golden Milk Bottle (High-Tier Dev Product):**
  * Guarantees a minimum **Super Cute** joey with a 25% chance of **Ultra Cute / Legendary**.

### B. Rescue Transport Upgrades
* **Multi-Crate Carrier (GamePass):**
  * Allows players to rescue and carry up to **3 or 5 Joeys simultaneously** per Forest trip, eliminating repetitive back-and-forth travel.
* **Forest Helicopter Drop (Dev Product or GamePass):**
  * Instant teleport straight to rare spawn locations deep within the forest.

## 🌿 3. Tycoon Acceleration & Automation GamePasses

### A. Core Multipliers
* **2x Park Revenue (Golden Eucalyptus Pass):**
  * Doubles all passive income generated by koalas across every exhibit.
* **2x Growth Speed (Super Eucalyptus Boost):**
  * Permanently cuts koala evolution times in half (stacks with the 1.5x cuddle boost).
* **Infinite Cuddle Energy:**
  * Eliminates the cuddle cooldown, letting players cuddle all koalas anytime for continuous happiness and speed bonuses.

### B. Automation Tools (High Player Value)
* **Auto-Feeder Drone:**
  * Automatically collects eucalyptus leaves and refills exhibit troughs whenever they dip below 50%.
* **Zookeeper Golf Cart / Safari Buggy:**
  * Drivable electric vehicle for navigating between distant exhibits, the Vet Clinic, and the Forest dock at 2.5x walk speed.

## 🔄 6. Rebirth & Prestige Synergy

* **Conservation Rebirth System:**
  * Once the sanctuary reaches full capacity, players can choose to "Endow the Sanctuary" (Rebirth).
